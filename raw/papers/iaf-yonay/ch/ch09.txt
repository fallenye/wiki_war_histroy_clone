   Freed of outside controls. Bar began moving at a dizzying pace
 and scope. Lacking theoretical grounding in aerial warfare   —   outside

defend the nation s airspace, maintain air superiority over enemy
forces, and assist the ground units. To carry out those missions, lAF
fighter pilots had to be proficient in both aerial defense and offense.
They had to be able to carry out escort, support, reconnaissance,
and photography missions, and to train new fighter pilots to do the
same. That was obvious.
   But nowhere was it ever specified how those goals were to be
accomplished. In fact, flying and gunnery skills even among the
